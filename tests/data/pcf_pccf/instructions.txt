Input data and counts for testing auto and cross correlations (pcf and pccf), with weights
==========================================================================================

data sample (d)   > pcf_gals_weight.fits
random sample (r) > pcf_rands_weight.fits
cross sample (c)  > pcf_gals1_weight.fits

pcf :
dd counts --> dd_pcf_weight.npy
rr counts --> rr_pcf_weight.npy

pccf :
cd counts --> cd_pccf_weight.npy
cr counts --> cr_pccf_weight.npy


Notes:
* data and random samples are just DATA.fits and RAND.FITS from the original repo example, with random normal weights added
* cross sample is a different sample of galaxies from SDSS, even with a different spatial coverage (i.e. cutted by different mask)
